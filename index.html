Against what you pay for

Under Ofcom's Broadband Speed Code of Practice your provider must give a guaranteed minimum download speed. If measured speed sits below it and they cannot fix it within 30 days, you can normally exit the contract without penalty. These figures are wired-to-router measurements from a Raspberry Pi; Wi-Fi speeds will be lower and are not evidence of a line fault.
